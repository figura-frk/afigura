Augmented workflow

How I use AI?

The design toolkit is being rewritten in real time — I'm constantly learning new tools and practices to optimise design work in the modern world, testing what genuinely speeds up the craft and dropping what doesn't.

Work process optimisation

Automating the repetitive layer of design work — renaming layers, generating variants, drafting documentation — so the hours go into decisions, not busywork.

Rapid prototyping

Turning an idea into a clickable prototype in hours with v0 and Framer AI — testing concepts with real users before committing a single sprint to them.

Research synthesis

Feeding interview transcripts and survey data to LLMs for first-pass clustering — then validating themes myself. Days of affinity mapping become an afternoon.

Copy & UX writing

Drafting microcopy, empty states, and error messages in the product's voice — AI proposes ten options, I pick and polish the one that sounds human.

Visual exploration

Generating moodboards, brand directions, and illustration styles with image models — widening the creative funnel before narrowing it with intent.

Design-to-code handoff

Bridging Figma and the codebase with AI — generating clean component code from designs so engineers review instead of rebuild, and nothing gets lost in translation.
